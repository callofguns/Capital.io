# Sound and music credits

Every sound and song in this folder is **CC0 (public domain)**. That means anyone can use it for anything, and no credit is needed.
We list where each one came from anyway, to say thanks to the people who made them.

Some files were trimmed, made mono, made evenly loud, and turned into MP3s so they're small and work on every phone.
